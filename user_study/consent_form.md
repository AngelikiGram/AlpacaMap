# Consent to take part in the AlpacaMap study

**Purpose.** We are evaluating AlpacaMap (called TravelScout in our publications), a research prototype that collects travel information
from several online sources and shows where they disagree. We want to learn how people use it to
plan a trip.

**What you will do.** Use the app for about 30 minutes to complete short planning tasks for two
cities, answer a few questionnaires and a short interview. Total time: about 50 to 60 minutes.

**What we record.**
- Your answers, task times and the buttons and views you click inside the app.
- If you agree below: screen and audio recording during the tasks.
- No name, e-mail or account information is stored with your answers. You are identified only
  by a participant number.

**Your rights.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason and
without any disadvantage. You can ask for your data to be deleted until it is anonymised for
publication.

**Risks and benefits.** There are no known risks beyond normal computer use. You will not benefit
directly, but you help improve tools for trip planning.

**Use of data.** Anonymised results may be published in scientific articles and shared as an open
dataset. Recordings are never published and are deleted after analysis.

**Data protection (GDPR).**
- *Controller:* Angeliki Grammatikaki (study lead), angiegram99@gmail.com.
- *Legal basis:* your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect
  processing done before the withdrawal.
- *Who sees the data:* only the study team. The app runs in the browser; the study page sends nothing to a server.
  The app loads maps and texts from public services (map tiles, OpenStreetMap, Wikimedia), which receive the
  study computer's IP address, not your name.
- *How long:* recordings are deleted after analysis, at the latest 6 months after your session. Pseudonymised
  answers are kept for 10 years (good scientific practice) and then deleted; anonymised data may be kept and
  published without time limit.
- *Your rights:* access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR).
  Contact the study lead with your participant number. You may also complain to the Austrian data protection
  authority (Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Wien, dsb.gv.at).
