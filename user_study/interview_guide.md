# Semi-structured interview (5 to 10 min, after the questionnaires)

Ask in this order; follow up with "Why?" / "Can you give an example?".

1. Walk me through how you found the disagreements in task 2. What did you look at first?
2. Was there a moment where seeing a disagreement changed your mind about a place? (RQ3)
3. Did you notice a difference between the two cities in what the app showed you? Did it matter? (RQ3, condition awareness)
4. Some statements are opinions, some are facts, some are tips like "go early in the morning". Should the app treat them differently? How? (RQ4)
5. When you planned the day, did you go back to the knowledge views? Why or why not? (RQ5)
6. Which parts of the app felt like too much? What would you remove first? (RQ2)
7. If you could keep only three views, which would they be? (RQ2)
8. Anything about privacy or where your data goes that you thought about during the session? (RQ6)

Note-taking: one line per answer with participant number, question number and a short quote.
Save as `data/P##_interview.md` (never commit with names).
